# Hospitalisation benefit

Carewell Plus covers eligible inpatient hospitalisation after a 30-day initial waiting period. Members should confirm the network hospital and pre-authorisation requirement before admission.

## Documents for a claim

Submit the health card, member ID, discharge summary, itemised hospital bill, prescriptions, and bank details.

## Before admission

A planned admission should be pre-authorised at least 48 hours before admission where possible.
